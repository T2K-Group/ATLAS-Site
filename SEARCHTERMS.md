# Image search terms

Use images with clear commercial licensing, no visible third-party branding, and enough negative space for responsive cropping. Prefer landscape images at least 1600px wide.

## Industries overview hero

Intended subject: an obviously important physical asset such as a sealed medical transport tote or blood-product transport container.

- `medical blood transport box secure logistics close up`
- `sealed medical specimen transport tote hospital logistics`
- `critical medical supplies transport container tracking`
- `temperature controlled blood transport container courier`
- `secure healthcare logistics tote operational photography`

Avoid graphic medical imagery, visible patient data, recognisable logos, and staged stock-photo handshakes.

## Public-sector hero — “Digital Chain of Custody for Critical Public-Sector Assets”

Intended subject: secure, accountable movement of important equipment between a vehicle, staff and a civic/operational building.

- `public sector secure equipment transport operational team UK`
- `government logistics secure asset handover UK`
- `civic operations equipment case vehicle handover`
- `secure ballot box transport polling station UK`
- `public service critical equipment logistics chain of custody`

Avoid identifiable ballot papers, party-political branding, police insignia, military imagery, or anything implying an existing ATLAS customer.

## Contact hero

Intended subject: a credible UK engineering/operations discussion, with room for page copy and no generic call-centre look.

- `UK engineering operations team planning meeting technology`
- `hardware software engineers reviewing logistics map office`
- `operations technology team product planning UK`
- `IoT engineering team discussing asset tracking dashboard`
- `British technology team collaborative workshop candid`

Avoid headset call centres, exaggerated posing, visible third-party software, and recognisable company logos.
