ATLAS by T2K

Critical Asset Tracking Built by T2K Group

ATLAS brings tracker hardware, connectivity and software together as one operational platform for organisations responsible for critical physical assets.

One connected product

Hardware and software designed together.

ATLAS is developed as a T2K Group product. Customers rent complete tracker units rather than selecting SIMs or managing tracker firmware themselves.

The platform is designed around practical operational questions: where is the asset, where has it been, what happened during its journey and what record is available afterward?

Why ATLAS exists

Operational visibility needs more than a consumer tracker.

ATLAS connects live reported location with physical-asset identity, status, movement history, user access and reporting so teams can work from a common operational record.

Engineering capability

Hardware, connectivity and software decisions can be considered as parts of the same operational system.

Use-case-led design

Deployment starts with the assets, journey, users, exceptions and evidence the organisation needs.

Clear accountability

Controlled access and retained history help authorised teams review movement and platform activity.

Physical-asset chain of custody

A digital record of a real-world journey.

In ATLAS, digital chain of custody refers to the retained electronic record of the movement, status and operational context of a physical asset. It is distinct from digital-forensics evidence handling and complements established physical custody processes.

What is a digital chain of custody for physical assets? →

Tracker → Connectivity → Platform

Available location and event information flows from the tracker through connectivity into the ATLAS dashboard, historical record and reporting tools.
