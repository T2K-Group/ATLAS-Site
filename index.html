ATLAS by T2K is a critical asset tracking platform that combines real-time location, condition, custody and activity into one auditable operational record.

One connected platform

From location ping to defensible proof

ATLAS combines the operational signals teams need to find assets, detect exceptions and demonstrate chain of custody without piecing together multiple systems.

Live asset visibility

See every connected tracker on an interactive map using multiple positioning methods, with current coordinates, accuracy, last-seen time and directions.

Sites and geofences

Define operational locations and know when a device enters, leaves or remains outside an approved site.

Condition monitoring

Monitor temperature, humidity, battery and cellular quality alongside physical button and enclosure events.

Secure, encrypted data

Protect operational location, condition and event data with secure access and encryption throughout the ATLAS platform.

Controlled team access

Invite users, manage licence seats and apply Viewer, Operator, Auditor, Administrator or Owner permissions.

Auditable operations

Review logins, configuration changes, exports, device events and security activity in one searchable record.
