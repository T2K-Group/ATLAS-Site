Agritech asset tracking

Operational Visibility for Agricultural Equipment and Remote Assets

ATLAS helps agricultural and agritech operations see the latest reported location of critical physical assets, retain movement history and work with intermittent connectivity across distributed sites.

Book a DemoDiscuss Your Operation
The operational problem

Equipment moves across wide areas, temporary sites and different teams.

When physical assets operate away from a permanent depot, location updates can depend on calls and local knowledge. That makes it harder to coordinate work, find equipment and review what happened after an exception.

Distributed physical assets

Machinery, tools and mobile equipment may move between fields, farms, contractors and stores.

Manual location checking

Teams can spend time calling colleagues or searching sites to establish where an asset was left.

Inconsistent connectivity

Remote areas can interrupt live updates and make historical retention especially important.

ATLAS capability

Connect remote asset visibility with retained operational history.

01

Latest reported location

View connected assets in one map and searchable dashboard rather than relying only on phone updates.

02

Sites and geofences

Define farms, depots, stores and working areas so location information reflects the operation.

03

Movement history

Review where a tracked physical asset travelled over a selected period.

04

Multi-network connectivity

Improve the opportunity to connect across available cellular networks as equipment moves.

05

Review supported battery, signal and environmental information alongside location where configured.

Agritech questions

Frequently asked questions

Can ATLAS work in remote areas?

Coverage varies. Multi-network connectivity improves the opportunity to connect, while retained location information supports later history when live service is interrupted.

Can ATLAS track farm machinery?

Potential suitability depends on the machinery, mounting, environment, reporting needs and operating process.

Does ATLAS replace machinery telematics?

Not necessarily. ATLAS focuses on operational asset visibility and movement history and may complement specialist machine systems.
