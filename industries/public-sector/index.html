The operational challenge

Accountability becomes harder when assets are moving.

Public-sector operations can span many people, temporary locations and time-critical handovers. ATLAS gives teams a shared operational picture while preserving the history needed after the event.

Know where assets are

Maintain visibility across sites and in transit without waiting for repeated phone calls or manual location updates.

Confirm successful arrival

Use current location and movement history to understand whether an asset has reached its intended destination.

Demonstrate accountability

Retain evidence that supports incident investigation, operational review and formal audit processes.

Specialist application

Election asset tracking has its own dedicated guidance.

ATLAS can add live visibility, arrival confirmation and retained movement history around ballot boxes and other critical election assets without replacing established election procedures.

The dedicated Elections section covers deployment, signal gaps, audit history and the relationship between digital visibility and physical custody processes.
