Election asset tracking

Digital Chain of Custody for Critical Election Assets

ATLAS helps election operations teams monitor ballot boxes and other critical physical assets, confirm arrival and retain an auditable movement history without replacing established election procedures.

Book a DemoDiscuss Your Election
The visibility problem

Critical election assets move across people, vehicles and locations.

Phone calls and manual updates can make it difficult to build a current shared picture. ATLAS adds a digital operational layer so authorised teams can see reported movement and focus attention on exceptions.

Ballot box visibility

View the latest reported location of ballot boxes and other election assets while they are in transit.

Arrival confirmation

Check whether a tracked asset has reached its expected count venue or operational destination.

Movement history

Retain a chronological physical-asset record for review after the live operation.

Existing-process compatibility

Digital visibility complements physical custody.

ATLAS does not replace sealing, transport, staffing, handover or escalation procedures. It supports those controls with a shared location view and retained history.

  • Keep established statutory and local procedures.
  • Add visibility without relying on consumer item finders.
  • Give appropriate users controlled platform access.
  • Retain an operational record for review and investigation.
Operational outcome

Fewer routine location calls

A shared election asset view can reduce repeated check-ins while helping the operations team recognise assets that require attention.

Resilient tracking

Preserve the journey through connectivity interruptions.

Multi-network connectivity is designed to improve the opportunity to connect across available mobile networks. When service is temporarily unavailable, the tracker can retain location information and upload it after connectivity returns.

Live visibility may pause during the gap, while retained information helps preserve the historical election asset movement record.

Election questions

Common questions about election asset tracking

Can ATLAS track ballot boxes?

ATLAS can provide reported location, arrival confirmation and retained history for ballot boxes and other defined election assets.

What happens without signal?

Location information can be retained during a temporary connectivity gap and uploaded when service returns.

Does ATLAS replace election processes?

No. It complements established custody and operational procedures with digital visibility and history.
