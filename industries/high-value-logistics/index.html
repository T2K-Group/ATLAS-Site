High-value asset tracking

Real-Time Visibility and Chain of Custody for High-Value Logistics

ATLAS helps authorised teams monitor valuable physical assets in transit, retain movement history and build an auditable operational record across sites and handover points.

The assurance problem

A current location alone does not explain a valuable asset’s journey.

High-value logistics teams may need a common view while assets move and a dependable history when a delay, route exception, disputed handover or other incident requires investigation.

Fragmented location updates

Calls, messages and spreadsheets can create conflicting accounts of where an important item was last seen.

Unclear movement history

A final delivery status does not show the route, timing or exceptions that occurred beforehand.

Difficult post-event review

Evidence can be slow to assemble when asset, location and platform activity are held separately.

High-value logistics questions

Frequently asked questions

Is ATLAS an anti-theft guarantee?

No. ATLAS provides visibility and history that can support security and response procedures; it does not guarantee prevention or recovery.

Can teams review an asset’s route?

Authorised users can review retained location and event history for the relevant asset and period.

What happens during a coverage gap?

Live updates may pause while location information is retained and later uploaded when connectivity returns.
