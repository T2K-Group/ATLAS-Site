Cold Chain Logistics Tracking with Location and Condition Context

ATLAS helps operations teams maintain visibility of temperature-sensitive physical assets, connect condition information with the journey and retain a record for review and investigation.

Book a DemoDiscuss Your Cold Chain
The operational problem

A temperature reading needs location, time and journey context.

Cold chain teams may need to understand where an asset is, whether it reached the expected site and what condition information was reported during transit. Separate calls, logs and systems make that sequence harder to review.

Limited in-transit visibility

Teams can lose a shared view when temperature-sensitive assets move between depots, vehicles and destinations.

Disconnected condition records

A reading is more useful when it can be reviewed alongside location, time and asset identity.

Slow exception investigation

Reconstructing a journey after an excursion or delay can require information from several people and systems.

Important boundary

Operational evidence, not automatic compliance

ATLAS information can support cold chain review. Suitability, calibration, validation, retention and regulatory compliance must be assessed for the specific product, process and deployed hardware.

Cold chain questions

Frequently asked questions

Does ATLAS monitor temperature?

ATLAS can display supported environmental telemetry where the selected tracker and service configuration provide it.

What happens if mobile service is lost?

Live information may pause. Location information can be retained temporarily and uploaded when connectivity returns.

Does ATLAS certify cold chain compliance?

No automatic compliance claim is made. The organisation must assess the complete process against its applicable requirements.
